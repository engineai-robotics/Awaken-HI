Introduction

Awaken-HI is a vision-language-action model for humanoid loco-manipulation that coordinates walking, posture, and hand movements. To scale data collection beyond robot teleoperation, we learn from first-person human demonstrations with synchronized whole-body motion. We reconstruct 3D hand motion from video and annotate demonstrations with subtask descriptions and per-frame depth maps. Because differences in body proportions and joints can alter postures and hand trajectories during retargeting, we retain both the original human motion and the retargeted robot actions. During pretraining, a shared action expert predicts both alongside camera-frame wrist poses. Separate heads predict current and future depth and describe the current subtask. We then post-train on about 150 hours of T800 teleoperation using the same robot action interface.

  • EngineAI-Human-50K pairs about 50,000 hours of egocentric stereo video with synchronized whole-body motion, captured using lightweight wearables under one protocol. It covers over 100 skills across homes, workplaces, retail, and storage.
  • From 5,000 to 50,000 pretraining hours, held-out action error falls by an empirical scaling law and mean success rises on three household tasks with fixed post-training data. Ablations show that original human motion supervision further lowers error and improves real-robot success.
  • With matched T800 post-training data, Awaken-HI surpasses GR00T N1.7 and π₀.₅ on all eight individual tasks (93.75% mean success) and leads on industrial and household long-horizon tasks. It generalizes to new pairings of familiar objects and destinations and to objects unseen during post-training.

Model

Qwen3-VL encodes the head-camera image and the task instruction. Those features condition one diffusion transformer by cross-attention. The T800 action, human whole-body and hand motion, and wrist branches share a state encoder and this transformer, so human trajectories train the backbone that generates robot actions. Wrist and depth prediction add supervision for interaction poses and scene geometry. The branch executed on the robot is the T800 action.

Pretraining data

EngineAI-Human-50K keeps 50,000 hours of whole-body egocentric demonstrations after deduplication and validity filtering: about 17.02 million episodes, 5.41 billion pose frames, and 11.18 million valid language instructions. A PICO headset and five body-worn trackers record stereo RGB and body motion together. Collectors follow the task and vary path, stance, and grasp, rather than repeating one fixed route.

Scaling egocentric data

We compare four nested pretraining runs (5k, 12.5k, 25k, 50k hours) on one held-out set. From 10% to 100% of the data, the T800 branch’s normalized prediction MSE over valid action dimensions drops ~11.0%, and validation flow-matching loss drops 18.8%. Power-law fits L(D) = AD^(−α) give α = 0.051 and 0.090, respectively. Smaller runs plateau and regress. The full run improves for longer.

Real-world evaluation

The charts compare Awaken-HI, π₀.₅, and GR00T N1.7 on seven individual tasks and two long-horizon tasks on T800. Each method trains one multitask policy on the same 150 hours of teleoperation. Each task has ten trials with shared initial configurations. Long-horizon success requires completing every step in order without a reset.
